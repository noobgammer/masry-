# Egyptian Arabic frequency vocabulary — Level 1 (ranks 1–100)

أنا | ana | I | pronoun | pronouns | انا | أنا من باكستان بس عشت في السعودية. | I'm from Pakistan but I lived in Saudi. | Often dropped when the verb already shows the person.
إنت | enta | you (m.) | pronoun | pronouns | انت | إنت منين أصلاً؟ | Where are you originally from? | Pronounced "enta" or "enti"/"entu" for f./pl.
إنتي | enti | you (f.) | pronoun | pronouns | انتي | إنتي فاضية النهارده؟ | Are you free today? | Spoken; in writing also انتي.
هو | huwwa | he / it | pronoun | pronouns |  | هو فين دلوقتي؟ | Where is he now? | Used for masculine things too: الأكل؟ هو جاهز.
هي | heyya | she / it | pronoun | pronouns |  | هي لسه في الشغل. | She's still at work. | Also used for feminine things and plurals of objects.
إحنا | e7na | we | pronoun | pronouns | احنا | إحنا رايحين السينما. | We're going to the cinema. | MSA نحن is never used in speech.
إنتو | entu | you (pl.) | pronoun | pronouns | انتو,انتم | إنتو جايين إمتى؟ | When are you (all) coming? | Written انتو or انتوا.
هما | humma | they | pronoun | pronouns | هم | هما قالولي إنهم جايين. | They told me they're coming. | MSA هم sounds formal.
ده | da | this / that (m.) | demonstrative | pronouns | دا | ده بتاعي. | This is mine. | Comes AFTER the noun: الكتاب ده = this book.
دي | di | this / that (f.) | demonstrative | pronouns |  | البنت دي صاحبتي. | This girl is my friend. | Also used for plural things: الحاجات دي.
دول | dool | these / those | demonstrative | pronouns |  | مين دول؟ | Who are those (people)? | Plural of people.
مش | mesh | not | negation | negation | مِش | أنا مش فاهم. | I don't understand. | Negates adjectives, participles, nouns, future: مش هروح.
لأ | la2 | no | particle | negation | لا,لاء | لأ، مش عايز. | No, I don't want (it). | لأ طبعاً = of course not.
آه | aah | yeah / yes | particle | expressions | اه,اها | آه، فاهم. | Yeah, I understand. | Very common casual yes; also "ouch".
أيوه | aywa | yes | particle | expressions | ايوة,ايوا | أيوه، أنا هنا. | Yes, I'm here. | Answering the phone: أيوه؟ = hello?

شاف / يشوف | shaaf / yeshoof | see / look | verb | verbs | شفت,شافت,شافوا,شفنا,يشوف,تشوف,نشوف,اشوف,شوف,شوفي,شايف,شايفة | شفت الماتش امبارح؟ | Did you see the match yesterday? | شوف = look!; شايف؟ = you see? (filler)

إدى / يدي | edda / yeddi | give | verb | verbs | اديت,ادت,ادوا,يدي,تدي,ندي,ادي,اديني,اديله | اديني الموبايل لو سمحت. | Give me the phone please. | Pronounced "eddi-ni".
